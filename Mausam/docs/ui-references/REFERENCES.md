# UI References — curated (replaces rival-clone screenshots, 03 Oct 2026)

Old PNGs (rival SIH repos) kept for honesty-checks only. Design from THESE:

## 1. Appy Weather — Google Play Best Apps, Everyday Essentials
- **Steal:** Timeline screen — the day in plain language ("what matters today"), not tiles. This is our decision-stack precedent.
- **Steal:** Routine subscriptions (weekday mornings, school runs) = persona scheduling; day-compare tool = our deterministic re-rank proof.
- **Steal:** Privacy-first (no personal data) = our guest-first + on-device personalisation.
- Source: https://www.thirdculture.app/appyweather

## 2. Hello Weather — App of the Day 2026
- **Steal:** "Simple on the surface, details one tap away" — exactly our provenance interaction. 12 data points, tap any card for the chart = tap any number for source/station/age.
- **Steal:** Source switching + 27 languages = our labelled adapters + template-first Hindi.
- **Steal:** Best-in-class dark mode that follows system settings.
- Source: https://www.helloweatherapp.com/

## 3. Overdrop — 1M+ users, clean widgets + 7-layer radar
- **Steal:** Widget discipline (glanceable first) + layered radar (one layer at a time) = our map overlay rule: one warning layer, labelled source.
- Source: https://get.overdrop.app/

## 4. UX4G 3.0 — the mandated authority (MeitY/NeGD)
- **Follow:** Mobile-first, Noto Sans tokens, 50+ components, accessibility widget (dark mode, text resize, high contrast), Hindi guidance, offline/caching guidance.
- Figma kit + CDN + Audit 360. Case studies: UMANG, DigiLocker, Poshan Tracker.
- Source: https://www.ux4g.gov.in/ · case studies https://www.ux4g.gov.in/community/case-studies

## 5. Dribbble weather tag + Figma community kits — browse, don't copy
- Glassmorphism/neumorphism concepts live here; our DESIGN.md bans glass-as-style. Take layout rhythm only.
- https://dribbble.com/tags/weather-app-ui

## Explicitly NOT references
- Purple-blue SaaS gradients, glass cards-as-style, decorative 3D icon sets (see DESIGN.md §4.9 anti-patterns).
